Frequently Asked Questions

How do I subscribe to ZNN News?

You can subscribe to our newsletters and breaking news alerts directly from the homepage. Our basic news feed is currently free to all readers.

How often is news updated?

ZNN is powered by a real-time AI system that monitors global feeds 24/7. News is updated continuously as events unfold, typically within minutes of a major lead.

Can I contribute articles to ZNN?

We are always looking for high-quality, fact-based reporting. If you are a journalist or a subject matter expert, please contact us via our Contact Page with a pitch or your portfolio.
